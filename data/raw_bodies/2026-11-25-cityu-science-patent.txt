Dear Principal / Teachers,



The *CityUHK Science Patent Challenge 2026-27*
<https://www.cityu.edu.hk/csci/local-school-outreach/cityuhk-science-patent-challenge>
provides secondary school students with opportunities to develop their
innovative science ideas and projects and explore how they can be
transformed into inventions with patent potential. Participants will
receive guidance from patent professionals, academics and industry experts.



*Key **Highlights*

*Two Entry Pathways*

   - *Emerging Idea Entry* for original science-based ideas that have not
   been publicly disclosed and have innovation and potential for a patent
   - *Developed Innovation Entry* for enhanced projects, prototypes or
   previous competition entries that demonstrate significant advancements
   beyond earlier versions, and with potential for a patent



*Learning and Expert Support*

*Online Expert Review Sessions*

*Exclusively f**or selected Developed Innovation Entries*

*Submission Link: **https://go.cityu.hk/wemw1g* <https://go.cityu.hk/wemw1g>
*(**Deadline: 30 November 2026**)*



Selected projects with strong innovation and patent potential will have the
opportunity to receive a* one-on-one review with a CityUHK professor,
patent professional, or industry expert* to explore technical improvements,
scientific validation, future development, and patent potential.




*Online Consultation Sessions **By appointment, up to two sessions per
individual/team*

*Registration Link: **https://go.cityu.hk/ishvs1*
<https://go.cityu.hk/ishvs1> (*Deadline: 11 December 2026**)*



Students can discuss their ideas and projects with our experts and *receive
advice to refine and strengthen their ideas*. *Nearly 50% of winning teams
have participated in the consultation sessions and provided positive
feedback*.





With these deadlines approaching, we encourage students to take advantage
of the Online Expert Review and Online Consultation Sessions to receive
expert feedback, refine their ideas, strengthen their projects, and explore
their potential for further development and a patent application.



Full competition details, eligibility requirements, timeline and support
opportunities are available on the competition website
<https://www.cityu.edu.hk/csci/local-school-outreach/cityuhk-science-patent-challenge>
.



We would greatly appreciate your help in sharing this reminder with your
Science/STEM teachers and interested students. We look forward to welcoming
aspiring young inventors from your school.



Yours sincerely,



College of Science
City University of Hong Kong

We are pleased to invite your students to participate in the fifth *CityUHK
Science Patent Challenge*
<https://www.cityu.edu.hk/csci/local-school-outreach/cityuhk-science-patent-challenge>,
co-organized by the Office of the Provost and Deputy President (OPVDP) and
the College of Science (CSCI), City University of Hong Kong (CityUHK).



The Challenge aims to introduce secondary school students to intellectual
property, innovation and scientific problem-solving. Through this STEM
competition, students will have opportunities to explore the innovation and
patent potential of their ideas while developing creativity,
problem-solving skills and entrepreneurial thinking.



*Highlights*

   - *Two Entry Pathways*


   - *Emerging Idea Entry* for original science-based ideas that have not
   been publicly disclosed and have innovation and potential for a patent
   - *Developed Innovation Entry* for enhanced projects, prototypes or
   previous competition entries that demonstrate significant advancements
   beyond earlier versions, and with potential for a patent


   - *Learning and Expert Support*


   - Intellectual Property Workshop delivered by a Patent Attorney
   - Online Consultation Sessions (by appointment, up to twice)
   - Online Expert Review Sessions (for selected Developed Innovation
   Entries only)


   - *Full fee sponsorship *of a Hong Kong Short-term Patent for Gold and
   Silver Award-winning teams
   - *Priority Admission Interview Opportunities *for all shortlisted
   participants of our flagship programme, Global Research Enrichment and
   Technopreneurship (GREAT)
   <https://www.cityu.edu.hk/csci/academic-programmes/undergraduate-programmes/global-research-enrichment-and-technopreneurship-great>,
   in their year of university admission



Full competition details, eligibility requirements, timeline and support
opportunities are available on the competition website
